## Neon Snake

A glowing, neon-styled Snake game that runs in any modern browser — no build step required.

**Play:** Open `index.html` in your browser.

**Controls:**
- Arrow keys or `W` `A` `S` `D` to steer
- `Space` to pause/resume

**Features:**
- Pink orbs grow you and add 10 points
- Golden orbs (appear occasionally, timed) give 25 points and ~4 seconds of rainbow invincibility that lets you wrap through walls
- Every 50 points, speed increases and level goes up
- Best score saved locally
